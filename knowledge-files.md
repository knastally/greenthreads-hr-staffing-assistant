# Knowledge Files

The GreenThreads HR Staffing Forecast Assistant was grounded using the following project materials.

## GreenThreads Case Brief
Provides the overall Denver launch context, operating constraints, staffing requirements, budget information, and company background.

## HR Functional Brief — HW#1
Defines the HR function, workflow, AI opportunities, and the Level 3 staffing-forecasting opportunity that this assistant was designed to implement.

## Launch & Activation Liaison Analysis — HW#2
Provides prior analysis connecting customer demand, store launch activity, and HR staffing needs.

## Denver Staffing Forecast Recommendation — HW#3
Provides the prior data analysis, choice set, recommendation, limitations, verification approach, and human decision-rights framework used to inform the assistant.

## Marketing B Customer Data
Contains customer acquisition channels, purchasing behavior, repeat purchases, spending, recency, email subscription status, and returns.

The cleaned analysis also includes validation checks, pivot-table analysis, charts, and supporting findings.

## Austin Store Daily Data
Provides historical daily store activity, including foot traffic, transactions, units sold, sales, returns, and other store-performance measures.

This data is used as historical evidence and should not be treated as a guaranteed prediction of Denver demand.

## HR Sales Associate Offer Letter
Provides hiring and onboarding context, including compensation, expected hours, start timing, and offer conditions.

## Denver Budget
Provides financial constraints and budget context relevant to staffing, recruiting, and technology decisions.

## Finance Spend Transactions
Provides information about recorded GreenThreads spending and commitments relevant to the Denver launch.

---

## Knowledge Use Rules

The assistant treats these files as its primary source of truth.

Historical data from other GreenThreads markets may be used as benchmarks or supporting evidence, but not as guaranteed Denver forecasts.

If the files do not contain enough information to answer a question, the assistant is instructed to state that limitation rather than invent missing information.

Important calculations should identify their source data and remain independently verifiable by a human reviewer.
